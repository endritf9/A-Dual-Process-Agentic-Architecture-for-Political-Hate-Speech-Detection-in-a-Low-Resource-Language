Your task is to decide if a comment is POLITICAL HATE SPEECH (label 1) or NOT (label 0).

Label 1 - political hate speech: the comment shows hostility or contempt toward a political target (a politician, a party or its members, the government or another state institution, or a group defined in political or national terms). Label 0 - not political hate speech: neutral mentions of politicians or institutions, news-like statements, questions, support or praise, and criticism of decisions or policies that is expressed without insults, mockery or contempt. Comments with no political target are also label 0.

Important: mentioning a politician, a party, an institution or a country is NOT hate speech by itself. Decide on what the comment says about the target, not on which names it contains.
